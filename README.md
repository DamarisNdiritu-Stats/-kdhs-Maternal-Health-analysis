 KDHS 2022 Maternal Health Service Utilisation Analysis

Project Overview

This project investigates factors associated with maternal health service utilisation among women in Kenya using data from the 2022 Kenya Demographic and Health Survey (KDHS).

The study applies descriptive statistics, bivariate analysis, and binary logistic regression to identify socio-demographic, economic, and accessibility factors associated with delivery in a health facility.

 Research Objective

 
 #General Objective

To model factors affecting maternal health service utilization in Kenya: logistic regression approach.
#Specific Objectives

1. To describe the socio-demographic and economic characteristics of women utilizing maternal health services.
2. To examine the relationship between accessibility factors and maternal health service utilisation.
3. To identify significant predictors of maternal health service utilisation using logistic regression.

# Dataset

The analysis uses data from the Kenya Demographic and Health Survey (KDHS) 2022.

The analysis focuses on variables including:

-Age group
- Education level
- Marital status
- Parity
- residence
- Wealth index
- Region
- Distance to facility
- Employment status
- Media exposure
-Facility delivery

The raw KDHS dataset is not included in this repository.

# Methodology

The analysis follows these stages:

 1. Data Cleaning

The data were cleaned and variables were recoded into appropriate formats for statistical analysis.

 2. Descriptive Analysis

Descriptive statistics were used to summarize the characteristics of the study population using frequencies, percentages, and appropriate graphical presentations.

 3. Bivariate Analysis

Associations between maternal health service utilisation and selected explanatory variables were examined using cross-tabulations and chi-square tests.

 4. Logistic Regression

Binary logistic regression was used to identify factors associated with facility delivery.

The dependent variable was coded as:

0 = Home delivery
1 = Facility delivery

Adjusted odds ratios (AORs), 95% confidence intervals, and p-values were used to assess the relationships between the predictors and facility delivery.

# Key Findings

The analysis indicated that several factors were associated with facility delivery.

- Education level was strongly associated with facility delivery.
-Women in the highest wealth category had higher odds of delivering in a health facility.
-Rural residence was associated with lower odds of facility delivery compared with urban residence.
-Increasing parity was associated with lower odds of facility delivery.
-Media exposure showed a positive but borderline association with facility delivery after adjustment.
-Age group and marital status were not statistically significant predictors after adjustment.
